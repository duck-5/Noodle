The Reality Check

Why does default Moodle feel so frustrating?

Tel Aviv University's Moodle is technically powerful, but its interface was designed over 15 years ago. Here's why students struggle with it every semester:

The 23:59 Deadline Panic

Deadlines are buried 4 folders deep in separate course pages. By the time you find where Assignment 3 is submitted, you're scrambling against the countdown clock.

Next-Up Spotlight: Real-time timer and 1-click submit right from your dashboard.

The Calendar Disconnect

You maintain your life in Google Calendar, but Moodle is an isolated island. You waste hours manually copying due dates — or worse, you forget one.

Native Google Tasks Sync: Zero-setup 2-way sync that lives in your calendar.

The Zoom Scramble

Is the lecture on Zoom today? Where is the link? You waste 10 minutes digging through syllabus text blocks and forum announcements trying to join.

Live Now Banner: 1-click join whenever a course session is active right now.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to common questions from TAU students.

Does Tel Aviv University officially make this?
No. Noodle is an independent, open-source productivity companion built by TAU students for TAU students. It interacts directly with the standard Moodle mobile web service and web endpoints that your browser already uses.
Is my TAU password or login data safe?
Yes, 100%. Noodle has no external servers or databases. Your login occurs directly through Tel Aviv University's official Single Sign-On portal (`nidp.tau.ac.il`). Noodle stores session cookies strictly inside Chrome/Firefox's encrypted local extension storage. We never see, track, or collect your credentials.
How does the Google Tasks synchronization work?
When you connect Google Tasks in settings, your browser requests an OAuth token directly from Google. Noodle creates a dedicated "University" task list in your account and syncs assignments client-side. No intermediate cloud service has access to your Google account.
Is there a mobile version available?
Yes! In addition to the Chrome and Firefox desktop browser extensions, the repository contains a native mobile app built with Expo and React Native for Android devices, sharing the exact same zero-backend sync engine.
Is Noodle free? Are there any hidden costs?
Noodle is completely free, open-source under the MIT license, and contains zero ads, subscriptions, or tracking. It is maintained out of passion to make university life easier.
